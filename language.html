GUIDE 01 — LANGUAGE

German language
for your medical future.

Strong German skills are central to safe communication with patients, colleagues and authorities. Build your learning plan around the requirements of the state where you intend to apply.

01 / GERMANPG854 GUIDE

Start with general German

Many doctors begin by developing general German before moving into professional and medical language. CEFR levels (A1 to C2) describe language proficiency, but a certificate alone does not automatically establish eligibility for medical practice.

02 / GERMANPG854 GUIDE

Move towards clinical communication

Medical German involves taking a structured history, explaining examinations, discussing diagnoses and treatment, documenting findings and handing over cases. Practise speaking clearly, listening actively and using patient-friendly language.

03 / GERMANPG854 GUIDE

Understand the FSP

The Fachsprachprüfung (FSP) is a professional language examination used in many licensing pathways. The exact format, required level, registration process and responsible body can vary by federal state. Check the relevant Ärztekammer or licensing authority.

04 / GERMANPG854 GUIDE

A practical weekly routine

Combine structured grammar and vocabulary study with daily listening, speaking practice, role-play consultations and short clinical summaries. Keep a vocabulary notebook organised by symptoms, anatomy, investigations, treatment and everyday patient communication.

05 / GERMANPG854 GUIDE

Official starting point

The Goethe-Institut provides German language courses and examinations. It is one possible learning resource; confirm which certificates and levels your competent authority accepts.

Important

This guide is a general educational overview, not legal, immigration or licensing advice. Your exact requirements depend on your qualification and the competent authority in the relevant federal state. Always verify current rules before acting.
