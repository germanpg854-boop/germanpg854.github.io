GUIDE 02 — RECOGNITION

Approbation &
medical recognition.

Approbation is the German licence to practise medicine without restriction. The route to it depends on where and how your medical qualification was obtained and the authority responsible for your application.

01 / GERMANPG854 GUIDE

What is Approbation?

Approbation is a state-issued professional licence that allows a physician to practise medicine in Germany on a permanent basis, subject to the applicable professional rules. It is distinct from a visa or residence permit.

02 / GERMANPG854 GUIDE

Who assesses your qualification?

Applications are handled by the competent authority in the German federal state where you apply. The authority assesses your documents and determines the applicable recognition route. Requirements, forms, fees and processing times can differ between states.

03 / GERMANPG854 GUIDE

Documents you may be asked for

Depending on your case, authorities may request identity documents, CV, medical diploma, transcripts or curriculum details, proof of professional registration or good standing, work experience evidence, health and character certificates, language evidence and certified translations. This is an illustrative list, not a universal checklist.

04 / GERMANPG854 GUIDE

Equivalence and the knowledge examination

The authority assesses whether your medical education is equivalent to the German standard. If substantial differences cannot be compensated for under the applicable rules, a Kenntnisprüfung may be required. The authority's written decision is the key reference for your individual case.

05 / GERMANPG854 GUIDE

Temporary permission

In some circumstances, a temporary Berufserlaubnis may be available under state law and subject to conditions. It is not the same as Approbation and does not provide an automatic entitlement to work. Ask the competent authority about eligibility, scope and duration.

06 / GERMANPG854 GUIDE

Plan before you apply

Choose the federal state carefully, read its current official checklist, confirm translation and certification rules, and keep copies of all correspondence. Do not send original documents unless the authority specifically instructs you to do so.

Important

This guide is a general educational overview, not legal, immigration or licensing advice. Your exact requirements depend on your qualification and the competent authority in the relevant federal state. Always verify current rules before acting.
