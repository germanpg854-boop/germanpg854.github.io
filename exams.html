GUIDE 03 — EXAMINATIONS

Prepare for the
medical examinations.

Two terms appear frequently in the recognition journey: Fachsprachprüfung (FSP) and Kenntnisprüfung (KP). They assess different things and may apply differently depending on your individual recognition decision and state.

01 / AEROMED GERMANY GUIDE

Fachsprachprüfung (FSP)

The FSP focuses on professional medical communication in German. It commonly assesses the ability to communicate with patients, discuss a case with medical colleagues and produce clinical documentation. The exact structure and administration depend on the responsible state medical chamber or authority.

02 / AEROMED GERMANY GUIDE

How to practise for the FSP

Practise patient interviews, structured history-taking, explaining diagnoses and procedures in accessible language, presenting cases to colleagues and writing concise medical notes. Use timed role-plays and ask a qualified German speaker or medical-language teacher for feedback.

03 / AEROMED GERMANY GUIDE

Kenntnisprüfung (KP)

The KP is a knowledge examination that may be required when the authority determines that the applicant's medical education has relevant differences that need to be assessed. It is not interchangeable with the FSP. The competent authority explains whether it applies and provides the relevant instructions.

04 / AEROMED GERMANY GUIDE

How to prepare for the KP

Build a structured revision plan around the official examination requirements and core clinical disciplines. Focus on clinical reasoning, diagnosis, differential diagnosis, investigations, treatment and patient safety. Use current recognised medical references and practise explaining your reasoning aloud.

05 / AEROMED GERMANY GUIDE

Confirm the rules for your state

Exam names, responsible bodies, application steps, waiting times and formats can change. Check the current information from your licensing authority and the relevant Landesärztekammer before paying for a course or booking an examination.

Important

This guide is a general educational overview, not legal, immigration or licensing advice. Your exact requirements depend on your qualification and the competent authority in the relevant federal state. Always verify current rules before acting.
