GUIDE 04 — SPECIALTY TRAINING

Find your path into
specialty training.

Medical specialty training in Germany is commonly referred to as Weiterbildung. It combines supervised clinical work with structured training requirements set by the relevant medical chamber.

01 / AEROMED GERMANY GUIDE

What is Weiterbildung?

Weiterbildung is postgraduate specialty training undertaken after the relevant professional requirements for medical practice have been met. Training requirements are governed by the applicable Weiterbildungsordnung of the responsible state medical chamber.

02 / AEROMED GERMANY GUIDE

Choose a specialty thoughtfully

Explore the clinical work, training duration, required competencies, rotations and long-term career options for each specialty. Talk to doctors working in the field and review the current training regulations rather than relying only on informal online summaries.

03 / AEROMED GERMANY GUIDE

Finding a position

Search hospital career pages, university hospital vacancies and reputable medical job portals. Tailor each application to the department and role. A clear CV, concise cover letter, evidence of language ability and relevant clinical experience can help communicate your readiness.

04 / AEROMED GERMANY GUIDE

Prepare your application

Keep your CV accurate and easy to scan. Include medical education, clinical rotations, employment history, research, language certificates and relevant courses. Prepare translated and certified documents where requested by the employer or authority.

05 / AEROMED GERMANY GUIDE

Understand the role of the medical chamber

The Landesärztekammer responsible for your training location is an important source for specialty training regulations, recognition of training periods and the process for obtaining a specialist title (Facharzt). Confirm details directly with the relevant chamber.

06 / AEROMED GERMANY GUIDE

Immigration and employment are separate

A professional licence, an employment contract and permission to live or work in Germany are separate matters. Your immigration route depends on your nationality and circumstances. Use official government information and obtain qualified advice where needed.

Important

This guide is a general educational overview, not legal, immigration or licensing advice. Your exact requirements depend on your qualification and the competent authority in the relevant federal state. Always verify current rules before acting.
